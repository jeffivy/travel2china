## How to Use the Planner

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| **1. Pick your duration** | Start with how many days you have. The itineraries above are organized by length |
| **2. Choose your template** | Copy the itinerary closest to your interests. Swap cities as needed |
| **3. Check travel times** | All city connections are listed with duration. High-speed trains: book 1–2 weeks ahead |
| **4. Adjust your pace** | 2 days per city is fast. 3 days is comfortable. 4+ days is slow travel |
| **5. Book in this order** | Flights → hotels → trains → attraction tickets (Forbidden City, Terracotta Warriors first) |

<div class="tip-box">
💡 <strong>Golden Rule of China Itineraries:</strong> Spend at least 2–3 nights in each city. Any less and you spend more time in transit than exploring. A 14-day trip covers 4–5 cities comfortably. If you try to pack 8 cities into 14 days, you'll remember train stations, not temples. Slow down. China will still be here next time.

## Tips for a Well-Balanced China Itinerary

| Tip | Why |
|-----|-----|
| **Alternate city types** | Follow a big city (Beijing) with a smaller one (Xi'an → Chengdu). Prevents megacity fatigue |
| **Build in a rest day** | Every 3–4 days, have a "zero day" — no planned activities. Sleep in. Sit in a teahouse for 3 hours. These are often the best days |
| **Start north, end south (or vice versa)** | Beijing → Xi'an → Chengdu → Guilin → Shanghai is the classic sequence — geographic logic without backtracking. [Full route →](/routes/golden-route) |
| **Book flexible middle days** | Lock in the first 2–3 days and the last day. Leave the middle flexible — you'll discover things you want to spend more time on |
| **Overnight trains save time** | A Beijing→Xi'an overnight train (12 hrs) costs half the high-speed fare AND saves a hotel night. Arrive at 7 AM, ready to explore |
| **Check opening days** | Forbidden City, Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, Nanjing Museum: closed Mondays. Plan around closures |
| **Fly the long gaps, train the short ones** | Train: <6 hours (Beijing–Xi'an, Xi'an–Chengdu). Fly: >6 hours or no direct train (Chengdu–Guilin) |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How to plan a trip to China?

Start with your available days. Pick 1 city per 2–3 days (10 days = 3–4 cities). Book in this order: international flights → visa → hotels → domestic trains → attraction tickets. Use the pre-built itineraries above as templates. [Full planning guide →](/routes/by-duration)

### How many days should I spend in each Chinese city?

**Beijing: 3–4 days** (Forbidden City + Great Wall + Temple of Heaven + hutongs). **Shanghai: 2–3 days** (Bund + French Concession + maybe Suzhou day trip). **Xi'an: 2–3 days** (Warriors + City Wall + Muslim Quarter). **Chengdu: 2–3 days** (Pandas + hot pot + tea house). **Guilin/Yangshuo: 2–3 days** (Li River + cycling). Smaller cities like Hangzhou, Suzhou, Nanjing: 2 days each.

### Is it better to fly or take the train between cities?

**Train for trips under 6 hours** (Beijing–Xi'an 4.5 hrs, Xi'an–Chengdu 3.5 hrs, Shanghai–Hangzhou 45 min). Trains are downtown-to-downtown, more reliable, and you can work or watch the scenery. **Fly for trips over 6 hours or with no direct train** (Chengdu–Guilin, Guilin–Shanghai).
