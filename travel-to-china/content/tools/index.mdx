## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are these tools really free?

Yes — all six tools are completely free. No sign-up, no email required, no hidden costs. Just open and use.

### Do the tools work offline?

The Travel Calendar, Packing List, and Phrase Cards work offline once loaded. The Quiz and Budget Calculator require an internet connection. The Itinerary Planner works offline after your route is generated.
