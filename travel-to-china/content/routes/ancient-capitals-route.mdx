# Ancient Capitals Route — 2,000 Years of Chinese History in 12 Days
 
 China's history was written in its capitals — dynasties rose and fell, emperors built palaces and tombs, and artists created treasures that still survive today. This route connects four of the most historically significant cities in Chinese civilization, each representing a different chapter in the story.
 
 **Beijing (3 nights) → Xi'an (3 nights) → Luoyang (2 nights) → Nanjing (3 nights)**
 
 ![Ancient capitals route map — Beijing, Xi'an, Luoyang, Nanjing connected by high-speed rail across eastern China](/images/routes-ancient-capitals-map.jpg)
 
---
 
 ## Why This Route
 
 | Reason | Why It Matters |
 |--------|---------------|
 | **4,000 years of history** | Every city represents a different dynasty: Ming/Qing (Beijing), Qin/Tang (Xi'an), Tang/Eastern Han (Luoyang), Ming/ROC (Nanjing) |
 | **High-speed rail** | Every leg is covered by fast trains. No flights needed. Total rail time: ~10 hours across four legs |
 | **UNESCO sites** | Forbidden City, Great Wall, Terracotta Warriors, Longmen Grottoes, Ming Xiaoling — five UNESCO sites in one trip |
 | **Easy progression** | The route makes a logical east-to-south sweep — no backtracking |
 | **Perfect for first-timers** | Every city on this route has excellent tourist infrastructure and English signage |

 ## Day-by-Day
 
 ### Day 1–3: Beijing (北京)
 
 **The imperial capital of the Ming and Qing dynasties**
 
 | Day | Activities |
 |-----|-----------|
 | **Day 1** | Forbidden City (morning) → Jingshan Park (panoramic view) → Wangfujing Night Market (evening) |
 | **Day 2** | Great Wall at Mutianyu (full day — 1.5h drive each way, cable car up, toboggan down) |
 | **Day 3** | Temple of Heaven (morning tai chi) → Summer Palace (afternoon) → Hutong rickshaw tour (evening) |
 
 **Must eat:** Peking duck (try Da Dong or Sijie Minfu), zhajiang noodles, jianbing.
 
 [Beijing Guide →](/cities/beijing)
 
 **To Xi'an:** High-speed rail from Beijing West to Xi'an North (4.5h, ¥515).
 
 ### Day 4–6: Xi'an (西安)
 
 **The first imperial capital — home of the First Emperor**
 
 | Day | Activities |
 |-----|-----------|
 | **Day 4** | Arrive → Ancient City Wall cycling (14km, rent a bike) → Muslim Quarter dinner |
 | **Day 5** | Terracotta Warriors (full day — 1h bus each way, 2–3h at the site) |
 | **Day 6** | Shaanxi History Museum (free, reserve ahead) → Big Wild Goose Pagoda → Muslim Quarter evening walk |
 
 **Must eat:** Biang biang noodles, lamb paomo, cold noodles (in the Muslim Quarter).
 
 [Xi'an Guide →](/cities/xian)
 
 **To Luoyang:** High-speed rail from Xi'an North to Luoyang Longmen (2.5h, ¥175).
 
 ### Day 7–8: Luoyang (洛阳)
 
 **The ancient capital of 13 dynasties — Buddhist art capital of China**
 
 | Day | Activities |
 |-----|-----------|
 | **Day 7** | Arrive → Longmen Grottoes (3–4h, afternoon light is beautiful on the Buddha statues) → Luoyang Old Town evening |
 | **Day 8** | White Horse Temple (morning, 45 min from city) or Shaolin Temple (full day, 1h drive) |
 
 **Must eat:** Luoyang Water Banquet, beef soup, peony pastry.
 
 [Luoyang Guide →](/cities/luoyang)
 
 **To Nanjing:** High-speed rail from Luoyang Longmen to Nanjing South (3.5h, ¥315).
 
 ### Day 9–11: Nanjing (南京)
 
 **The southern capital — Ming Dynasty grandeur and Republican-era elegance**
 
 | Day | Activities |
 |-----|-----------|
 | **Day 9** | Arrive → Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum (392 steps, morning) → Ming Xiaoling Spirit Way (afternoon) |
 | **Day 10** | Nanjing Museum (Republican Hall — best museum experience in China) → Presidential Palace → Confucius Temple area evening |
 | **Day 11** | Yangtze River Bridge walk → Xuanwu Lake → Jiming Temple |
 
 **Must eat:** Salted duck, duck blood vermicelli soup, beef pot stickers.
 
 [Nanjing Guide →](/cities/nanjing)
 
 **Departure:** Nanjing has direct flights to most international destinations. Alternatively, take the train to Shanghai Hongqiao (1h, ¥135) for more international flight options.

 ## Important Notes
 
 - **Golden Week:** Avoid the first week of October (National Day) and May (May Day) — all four cities are packed
 - **Train tickets:** Book 14 days ahead for popular legs (especially Beijing→Xi'an and Xi'an→Nanjing)
 - **Nanjing Airport:** NKG (Lukou) has international flights to Japan, Korea, and Southeast Asia. For trans-Pacific or European flights, take the 1h train to Shanghai Pudong
 - **Luoyang vs Shaolin:** The Shaolin Temple day trip from Luoyang takes a full day (1h each way). If you're short on time, skip it and spend more time at the Longmen Grottoes
 - **Nanjing Museum:** The Republican Hall (a recreated 1930s street) requires a free reservation 1–3 days ahead — don't miss it
