# Yunnan Classic Route — Southern China's Most Beautiful Province in 10 Days
 
 Yunnan is China's most geographically and culturally diverse province — snow-capped mountains, tropical rainforests, rice terraces, and ancient towns inhabited by 25 different ethnic minority groups. This route takes you from the provincial capital through ancient lakeside towns, a legendary gorge, and up to the Tibetan Plateau.
 
 **Kunming (2 nights) → Dali (2 nights) → Lijiang (3 nights) → Shangri-La (2 nights)**
 
 ![Yunnan route map — Kunming, Dali, Lijiang, Shangri-La connected by railway and highway](/images/routes-yunnan-map.jpg)
 
---
 
 ## Why This Route
 
 | Reason | Why It Matters |
 |--------|---------------|
 | **Unmatched diversity** | Lakes, mountains, gorges, ancient towns, and Tibetan culture in one trip |
 | **High-speed rail connected** | Kunming → Dali → Lijiang by HSR (2h each). Only Shangri-La requires a bus |
 | **Altitude progression** | Gradual ascent: Kunming (1,900m) → Dali (1,970m) → Lijiang (2,400m) → Shangri-La (3,300m) |
 | **Ethnic diversity** | Han, Bai, Naxi, Yi, and Tibetan cultures all along the route |
 | **Food variety** | Yunnan's cross-bridge noodles, Bai cuisine, Naxi specialties, and Tibetan yak dishes |

 ## Day-by-Day Itinerary
 
 ### Day 1–2: Kunming (昆明)
 
 **The "Spring City" — Yunnan's capital at 1,900m**
 
 | Activity | Details |
 |----------|---------|
 | **Stone Forest (石林)** | UNESCO karst landscape 90 min from Kunming. ¥130. Half day |
 | **Green Lake Park (翠湖)** | Free. City-centre lake with thousands of seagulls in winter |
 | **Yunnan Provincial Museum** | Free. Excellent ethnic minority exhibits |
 | **Flower Market (斗南花市)** | Asia's largest flower market. Best at night |
 
 **Must eat:** Crossing-the-bridge noodles (过桥米线). Wild mushroom hotpot in July-August.
 
 **Transport to Dali:** High-speed rail from Kunming Station (2h, ¥145).
 
 ### Day 3–4: Dali (大理)
 
 **Erhai Lake, Three Pagodas, and Bai culture at 1,970m**
 
 | Activity | Details |
 |----------|---------|
 | **Erhai Lake cycling** | 30km Dali → Xizhou along the lake. Bike ¥30-50/day |
 | **Three Pagodas** | ¥75. 1,200-year-old Tang Dynasty pagodas |
 | **Dali Ancient Town** | Free. Bai architecture, cafes, and craft shops |
 | **Cangshan Mountain cable car** | ¥120-200. Views over Erhai from 3,800m |
 
 **Must eat:** Erkuai (rice cakes), Yunnan ham, rose cake.
 
 **Transport to Lijiang:** High-speed rail (2h, ¥65).
 
 ### Day 5–7: Lijiang (丽江)
 
 **UNESCO Old Town, Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, and Naxi culture at 2,400m**
 
 | Activity | Details |
 |----------|---------|
 | **Lijiang Old Town** | Free. UNESCO World Heritage. Cobblestone lanes and flowing canals |
 | **Jade Dragon Snow Mountain** | ¥100 + ¥120-180 cable car. Glacier at 4,680m |
 | **Black Dragon Pool** | Free. Iconic reflection of the snow mountain |
 | **Shuhe Ancient Town** | Free. Quieter, less commercialised old town |
 | **Tiger Leaping Gorge day trip** | One of the world's deepest gorges (3,790m) |
 
 **Must eat:** Naxi baba flatbread, Yunnan ham, grilled tilapia.
 
 **Transport to Shangri-La:** Bus from Lijiang (3.5h, winding mountain roads).
 
 ### Day 8–10: Shangri-La (香格里拉)
 
 **Tibetan plateau town at 3,300m**
 
 | Activity | Details |
 |----------|---------|
 | **Songzanlin Monastery (松赞林寺)** | ¥90. "Little Potala Palace." Active Tibetan Buddhist monastery |
 | **Pudacuo National Park (普达措)** | ¥138. Alpine lakes, meadows, and forests |
 | **Dukezong Ancient Town (独克宗)** | Free. 1,300-year-old Tibetan old town. The world's largest prayer wheel |
 | **Tibetan family visit** | Book through your hotel. Yak butter tea, tsampa, and hospitality |
 
 **Must eat:** Yak hotpot, butter tea, tsampa, Tibetan momos.

 ## Important Notes
 
 - **Altitude:** Shangri-La is at 3,300m. Spend at least 1-2 nights in Lijiang (2,400m) before heading up to acclimatise
 - **Best time:** March–May for spring blossoms; September–November for clearest mountain views
 - **Avoid:** Chinese National Day (Oct 1-7) and May Day (May 1-5) — Lijiang and Dali are packed
 - **Tiger Leaping Gorge:** The High Trail trek is 2 days. Casual visitors can do a day trip from Lijiang
 - **Visa:** Shangri-La is in Yunnan, not Tibet — no Tibet Travel Permit needed
