# Silk Road Route — Following the Ancient Caravan Path from Xi'an to Kashgar
 
 The Silk Road was not a single road but a network of trade routes connecting China to Central Asia, Persia, and Europe for over 1,500 years. This itinerary follows the most historically significant Chinese section of the route — from the ancient capital where the road began, through the desert oasis towns that made it legendary, to the crossroads of Central Asia at China's western frontier.
 
 **Xi'an (3 nights) → Dunhuang (2 nights) → Turpan (2 nights) → Kashgar (3 nights)**

 ## Day-by-Day Itinerary
 
 ### Day 1–3: Xi'an (西安)
 
 **The Silk Road's eastern terminus — 3,000 years of history**
 
 | Activity | Details |
 |----------|---------|
 | **Terracotta Warriors (兵马俑)** | ¥120. The 8th Wonder of the World. 2h from city |
 | **Ancient City Wall** | ¥54. 14km cycling circuit on the best-preserved city wall in China |
 | **Muslim Quarter (回民街)** | Free. Hui Muslim food street. Lamb skewers, biang biang noodles, pomegranate juice |
 | **Shaanxi History Museum** | Free (reserve). Top-3 museum in China. Tang Dynasty gold treasures |
 | **Great Mosque (大清真寺)** | ¥25. 1,300-year-old fusion of Chinese and Islamic architecture |
 
 **Must eat:** Biang biang noodles, lamb paomo (bread soup), cold noodles (凉皮).
 
 **Transport to Dunhuang:** Flight (2h, ¥500-1,200) or overnight train (12h, ¥250-500).
 
 ### Day 4–5: Dunhuang (敦煌)
 
 **The desert oasis — Mogao Caves and Crescent Moon Spring**
 
 | Activity | Details |
 |----------|---------|
 | **Mogao Caves (莫高窟)** | ¥238. 492 cave temples with 1,000 years of Buddhist art. Book 3-7 days ahead |
 | **Crescent Moon Spring (月牙泉)** | ¥120. Desert oasis among Mingsha Sand Dunes |
 | **Yumen Pass (玉门关)** | ¥90. Han Dynasty frontier fortress in the Gobi Desert |
 | **Shazhou Night Market** | Free. Lamb skewers, apricot peel tea, souvenirs |
 
 **Must eat:** Lanzhou beef noodles, lamb skewers, apricot peel tea.
 
 **Transport to Turpan:** Overnight train (8h, ¥200-400).
 
 ### Day 6–7: Turpan (吐鲁番)
 
 **The oasis below sea level — vineyards and Uyghur culture**
 
 | Activity | Details |
 |----------|---------|
 | **Jiaohe Ruins (交河故城)** | ¥70. 2,300-year-old Silk Road city ruins |
 | **Flaming Mountains (火焰山)** | ¥40. Red sandstone range that inspired Journey to the West |
 | **Karez Irrigation System (坎儿井)** | ¥40. Ancient underground water channels (UNESCO) |
 | **Grape Valley (葡萄沟)** | ¥60. Uyghur village in a lush valley |
 
 **Must eat:** Fresh Turpan grapes (August-October), Uyghur pilaf, lamb kebab, naan bread.
 
 **Transport to Kashgar:** Overnight train (12h, ¥300-500).
 
 ### Day 8–10: Kashgar (喀什)
 
 **The Silk Road's western crossroads — Uyghur culture at its most authentic**
 
 | Activity | Details |
 |----------|---------|
 | **Id Kah Mosque (艾提尕尔清真寺)** | ¥30. China's largest mosque. 500-year-old Uyghur architecture |
 | **Kashgar Old City (喀什古城)** | Free. UNESCO-listed maze of mud-brick houses and 500 alleyways |
 | **Sunday Bazaar (周日巴扎)** | Free. Central Asia's largest market. Livestock, carpets, spices, and everything else |
 | **Apakh Hoja Tomb (香妃墓)** | ¥30. 17th-century mausoleum with turquoise tiles |
 
 **Must eat:** Lamb pilaf (polo), laghman noodles, samsa (烤包子), naan, Uyghur tea.
 
 **Transport back:** Flight from Kashgar to Urumqi (2h) → connect to Beijing/Shanghai/Guangzhou.

 ## Important Notes
 
 - **Long distances:** This route involves four flights or overnight trains. Plan for travel days between cities
 - **Mogao Caves:** Tickets MUST be booked 3-7 days in advance. Only 6,000 visitors allowed per day
 - **Best time:** September–October for the most comfortable temperatures across all four destinations
 - **Food:** Kashgar's cuisine is Uyghur (Central Asian) — very different from Chinese food. Lamb, naan, and pilaf dominate
 - **Xinjiang travel:** Foreigners need to register their accommodation with the local PSB within 24 hours. Your hotel handles this
 - **Climate:** Dunhuang and Kashgar are extremely dry. Drink 2-3L of water per day. Carry moisturiser and lip balm
