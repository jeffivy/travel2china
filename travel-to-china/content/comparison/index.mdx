## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which comparison should I use first?

Start with the **[Ultimate City Comparison Tool →](/comparison/cities/)** — it lets you filter across multiple dimensions simultaneously. Once you've narrowed to 2–3 cities, use the specific comparison pages to make your final call.

### Where does the data come from?

All data is sourced from publicly available climate records (China Meteorological Administration), real-time cost data from Trip.com, Dianping, and 12306, and traveler surveys conducted by our editorial team. We update comparison tables quarterly.

### Can I compare cities that aren't on the main city list?

Our comparison tools cover all 16 cities in our [city guides →](/cities). Smaller cities and rural destinations are covered in the individual city guides but not yet in the comparison tools. We're adding more destinations each quarter.
