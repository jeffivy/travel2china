# 10 Days in China - The Perfect First-Timer's Itinerary

Landing in China for the first time can feel overwhelming - 1.4 billion people, a script you can't read, apps you've never used. But the country's **Golden Route** (Beijing - Xi'an - Guilin - Shanghai) was designed exactly for this. It hits all the iconic sights, each city is connected by high-speed rail or a short flight, and ten days is the sweet spot to see it all without burnout.

## Before You Go

Before we dive into the itinerary, make sure you've covered the essentials:

- **Visa**: Most nationalities need a tourist (L) visa. Check our [Visa Tourist Guide](/country/visa-tourist-guide) for the application process.
- **Apps**: Install **Alipay** (for payments), **WeChat** (for messaging and payments), **DiDi** (ride-hailing), and a **VPN** before you arrive. Our [China Travel 101 guide](/blog/china-travel-101) has a full setup walkthrough.
- **Packing**: Late autumn / winter layers depending on your travel month. See [Packing List](/tools/packing-list) for a complete checklist.

## The 10-Day Golden Route

| Day | City | Highlights |
|-----|------|------------|
| 1 | Beijing | Arrive, settle in, evening Wangfujing night market |
| 2 | Beijing | Forbidden City + Jingshan Park + Hutongs |
| 3 | Beijing | Great Wall (Mutianyu) - full-day trip |
| 4 | Beijing - Xi'an | Morning Temple of Heaven, afternoon high-speed train (4.5h) |
| 5 | Xi'an | Terracotta Warriors + Muslim Quarter |
| 6 | Xi'an - Guilin | City Wall cycling (morning), fly to Guilin (2.5h) |
| 7 | Guilin | Li River cruise to Yangshuo + West Street |
| 8 | Yangshuo | Cycling countryside + Moon Hill + evening Impression show |
| 9 | Yangshuo - Shanghai | Morning free, fly Guilin - Shanghai (2.5h), evening Bund walk |
| 10 | Shanghai | The Bund (day), Yu Garden, Huangpu River cruise, departure |

---

### Day 1: Arrive in Beijing

Most international flights land at **Beijing Capital (PEK)** or **Beijing Daxing (PKX)**. After customs and baggage, take the Airport Express to the city center (about 30?60 mins).

**Where to stay:** Dongcheng District (near Wangfujing or Gulou) - walking distance to Forbidden City and Hutongs.

**Evening:** Visit **Wangfujing Night Market** for skewers, candied hawthorn, and Beijing?? (yogurt). It's touristy but fun, and a gentle introduction to Chinese street food.

---

### Day 2: Forbidden City & Hutongs

**Morning (4 hours):** The **Forbidden City** deserves at least half a day. Book tickets online at least 3 days ahead - they sell out. Walk south to north through the Gate of Heavenly Peace, the Outer Court (grand halls), and the Inner Court (imperial living quarters).

**Lunch:** Try Peking duck nearby at a local restaurant (not the expensive ones targeting tourists).

**Afternoon:** Climb **Jingshan Park** (directly north of the Forbidden City) for the iconic rooftop view. Then walk through the **Hutong** alleyways around Nanluoguxiang - Beijing's traditional neighborhood with courtyard homes, boutique shops, and tiny restaurants.

**Evening:** [Beijing Travel Guide ?](/cities/beijing)

---

### Day 3: The Great Wall

**Full day:** Take a bus or private car to **Mutianyu** - the best-restored section for first-time visitors, about 1.5 hours from the city. It's less crowded than Badaling and has a toboggan ride down.

**Pro tip:** Go on a weekday. Arrive by 8:00 AM to beat the tour groups. Bring water and snacks - the food at the base is overpriced.

---

### Day 4: Beijing - Xi'an

**Morning:** Visit the **Temple of Heaven** - a stunning Ming-era temple complex where emperors once prayed for good harvests. See locals practicing Tai Chi and singing opera in the park.

**Afternoon:** Take the **G-series high-speed train** from Beijing West to Xi'an North (4.5 hours, ?515 second class). Book your ticket on **12306** (China's official railway site) or have your hotel concierge help.

**Evening:** Xi'an's ancient City Wall is beautifully lit at night. Walk the Muslim Quarter for lamb skewers and pita-bread-soaked-in-soup (*yangrou paomo*).

---

### Day 5: Terracotta Warriors

**Morning:** The **Terracotta Warriors** are 1 hour east of Xi'an. Three pits, but Pit 1 is the showstopper - 6,000 life-sized soldiers in battle formation. Give yourself 3 hours.

**Afternoon:** Back in the city, explore the **Muslim Quarter** - a warren of food stalls, spice shops, and mosques. Try *biangbiang* noodles and persimmon cakes.

**Evening:** [Xi'an Travel Guide ?](/cities/xian)

---

### Day 6: Xi'an - Guilin

**Morning:** Rent a bike and cycle the **Xi'an City Wall** (14 km, about 2 hours). It's the most intact ancient city wall in China and offers great views of the old city vs. the modern skyline.

**Afternoon:** Fly from Xi'an Xianyang (XIY) to Guilin Liangjiang (KWL) - about **2.5 hours**, ?600?1,000. Alternatively, take the high-speed train (6.5 hours) if you prefer rails to wings.

**Evening:** Walk along the **Two Rivers and Four Lakes** - Guilin's central waterways are beautifully lit at night.

---

### Day 7: Li River Cruise

**The highlight of your trip:** A 4-hour cruise from Guilin to **Yangshuo** down the Li River - the same landscape printed on the ?20 note. Karst peaks rise from misty waters like dragons' teeth.

**Arrive in Yangshuo** by mid-afternoon. Check into your guesthouse near West Street and wander the boutiques and caf?s.

**Evening:** [Guilin Travel Guide ?](/cities/guilin)

---

### Day 8: Yangshuo Countryside

**Morning:** Rent an e-bike or bicycle and cycle the countryside trails - through rice paddies, past Moon Hill, and along the Yulong River. This is the China you came for: water buffalo, old stone bridges, and farmers in conical hats.

**Afternoon:** Visit **Moon Hill** - climb the 800 steps for a panoramic view of the karst landscape.

**Evening:** The **Impression Liu Sanjie** show - a spectacular outdoor performance directed by Zhang Yimou, set against the actual karst mountains as a backdrop. Book tickets in advance.

---

### Day 9: Yangshuo - Shanghai

**Morning:** Last swim or countryside walk. Pick up some local tea or bamboo rice as souvenirs.

**Afternoon:** Bus back to Guilin (1.5 hours), then fly to **Shanghai Pudong (PVG)** - about **2.5 hours**, ?700?1,200.

**Evening:** Walk the **Bund** at night - the iconic skyline: colonial-era buildings on one side, Pudong's futuristic towers on the other. The view never gets old.

---

### Day 10: Shanghai & Departure

**Morning:** Visit **Yu Garden** (a Ming dynasty classical garden in the old city) and **City God Temple** area for xiaolongbao (soup dumplings) at the famous Din Tai Fung.

**Afternoon:** Take a **Huangpu River cruise** for a final water-level view of the Pudong skyline. Head to the airport 3 hours before your flight.

**Departure:** Most international flights leave from **Shanghai Pudong (PVG)**. The Maglev train gets you there in 8 minutes from Longyang Road station.

**Last stop:** [Shanghai Travel Guide ?](/cities/shanghai)

## When to Take This Trip

This itinerary works year-round, but the sweet spots are:

- **April?May**: Spring blooms, comfortable temperatures
- **September?November**: Clear skies, autumn colors, fewer crowds
- Avoid **October 1?7** (Golden Week) and **Chinese New Year** (late Jan/early Feb)
