# China in July 2026 – Where to Go, Weather & Travel Tips

July is China's hottest and most humid month — but it's also when the country's high-altitude destinations, grasslands, and northern coastlines are at their absolute best. The key is knowing where to go (and where absolutely not to go).

## July Weather at a Glance

| Region | Temp (°C) | Conditions |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Beijing / North China | 25–38 | Hot, humid, occasional thunderstorms |
| Shanghai / East China | 27–37 | Oppressive humidity, plum rain ending |
| Guangzhou / South China | 28–35 | Hot, typhoon risk |
| Kunming / Yunnan | 17–26 | Perfect — spring-like, occasional rain |
| Tibet / Western Sichuan | 10–22 | Ideal — dry, sunny, cool |
| Inner Mongolia | 18–30 | Warm, green grasslands |

## Best Cities to Visit in July

| City | Why Go | Temperature |
|------|--------|------------|
| [Kunming](/cities/kunming) | "City of Eternal Spring" — 17–26°C year-round. Wild mushroom season peaks. The best summer escape in China | 17–26°C |
| [Lijiang](/cities/lijiang) | High-altitude cool weather (2,400m). Tiger Leaping Gorge hiking. Ancient Tea Horse Road town. Book ahead | 15–24°C |
| [Harbin](/cities/harbin) | Surprisingly pleasant in summer — 20–28°C. Russian architecture, beer gardens. The summer music festival runs through July | 18–28°C |
| [Zhangjiajie](/cities/zhangjiajie) | The Avatar Mountains in summer mist — lush, dramatic, otherworldly. Hot (25–33°C) but the scenery justifies it. Go early morning | 25–33°C |
| [Qingdao](/cities/qingdao) | Beaches, sea breezes, and the Tsingtao Beer Festival in early August. Book hotels 2+ months ahead for the festival | 23–30°C |

## Where to Avoid in July

| City | Why Skip |
|------|---------|
| [Beijing](/cities/beijing) | "Furnace weather" — 35–38°C with humidity. The Forbidden City has no shade. The Great Wall in August is an endurance test |
| [Shanghai](/cities/shanghai) | Oppressive humidity. The French Concession is pleasant in the evening but daytime sightseeing is miserable |
| [Nanjing](/cities/nanjing) | One of China's "Three Furnaces" — extremely hot and humid |
| [Xi'an](/cities/xian) | Hot, dry, and dusty. The Terracotta Warriors pits have air conditioning but the city itself is unpleasant |

## July Events & Festivals

| Event | Dates | Where |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Naadam Festival (Mongolian Games) | Mid-July | Inner Mongolia grasslands |
| Wild Mushroom Season | Jun–Sep | Kunming, Dali, Yunnan |
| Harbin Summer Music Festival | Jul–Aug | Harbin |
| Qingdao Beer Festival | Early Aug | Qingdao (some pre-events in late July) |

## Tips for Traveling in July

- **Book high-altitude destinations early** — Kunming, Lijiang, Shangri-La, Tibet fill up with domestic tourists escaping the heat
- **Pack for rain** — July is monsoon season in southern and eastern China. Bring a lightweight rain jacket and waterproof shoes
- **Go early** — Start outdoor sightseeing by 8 AM, rest during the midday heat, go out again after 4 PM
- **Stay hydrated** — Buy bottled water. Tap water is not safe anywhere in China
- **Check typhoon forecasts** — Coastal cities (Shanghai, Guangzhou, Xiamen) can experience flight and ferry cancellations during typhoon warnings
