# China in October 2026 – Golden Autumn, National Day & Perfect Weather

October is China at its most photogenic. The ginkgo trees of Beijing and Xi'an turn gold. The maple of Fragrant Hills and Ming Xiaoling blazes red. The skies are the clearest of the year across northern and central China. There's just one problem: **National Day Golden Week** (October 1–7), when the entire country travels and every attraction reaches capacity.

The strategy: stay home during Golden Week (Oct 1–7), then travel from October 8–31 for the best conditions of the entire year.

## The Golden Week Strategy

Golden Week (Oct 1–7) is a hard NO for spontaneous travel. Every scenic spot is packed. Every train is sold out. Every hotel is triple price.

But if you travel **October 8–31**, you get:
- Post-Golden Week hotel discounts (prices drop 30–50% on Oct 8)
- Comfortable temperatures without summer heat
- Golden foliage at peak in late October
