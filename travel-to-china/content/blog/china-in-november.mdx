# China in November 2026 - Autumn Colors, Fewer Crowds & Perfect Hiking

November is China's secret sweet spot. The summer crowds are gone. The October Golden Week chaos is a distant memory. The air is crisp, the skies are clear, and autumn foliage reaches its fiery peak across the central and southern regions. For travelers who value comfortable weather and photogenic landscapes without the crowds, this might be **the best month of the year to visit**.

## Best Places to Visit in November

### Nanjing - China's Autumn Capital

By early November, Nanjing's famous?? (plane tree) avenues turn a deep amber, and the **Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum's Spirit Way** is framed by blazing red and gold leaves. The city's classical gardens and Confucius Temple area are at their most photogenic. Don't miss the Qinhuai River at dusk.

**Why go:** The most spectacular autumn foliage in eastern China, with far fewer tourists than Beijing.

### Huangshan - Cloud Seas and Sunrise

November brings the famous **Huangshan cloud seas** - layers of mist that roll through the granite peaks, creating a scene straight from a classical Chinese painting. The hiking is crisp and comfortable, though you'll need a coat at the summit. Skip the?? week crowds and go mid-month for the best experience.

**Why go:** November offers the most dramatic cloud sea views of the year.

### Guilin & Yangshuo - Perfect Hiking Weather

October's warmth lingers into November in Guangxi. The Li River is clear, the rice terraces at Longji are golden brown (post-harvest texture), and the karst peaks of Yangshuo offer **perfect hiking temperatures** - 18?22?C with low humidity. Cycle the countryside trails without breaking a sweat.

**Why go:** Arguably the most comfortable month for outdoor activities in southern China.

### Kunming & Yunnan - Perpetual Spring

November is one of Kunming's best months - sunny days, blue skies, and temperatures that hover around a perfect 15?20?C. The rice terraces of Yuanyang begin to fill with water (late November), and the old town of Lijiang is peaceful without summer crowds. Dali's Erhai Lake is stunning under clear autumn light.

**Why go:** "Spring City" at its springiest. Sunny every day.

### Beijing - Quiet Forbidden City

Beijing in November is cold but clear. The real advantage: **the Forbidden City, Summer Palace, and Great Wall are thinly visited**. You can walk through the Forbidden City's grand courtyards without being jostled. The Great Wall at Mutianyu on a clear November weekday is an almost private experience.

**Why go:** The most comfortable time to visit Beijing's top attractions without crowds.

## What to Pack

- **North (Beijing, Xi'an):** Warm layers - thermal base, sweater, mid-weight jacket. Gloves and scarf for late November.
- **Central (Nanjing, Shanghai, Huangshan):** Light jacket or fleece. Rain layer for Huangshan summit.
- **South (Guilin, Yunnan):** Light layers - t-shirt plus light jacket for evenings.
- **Footwear:** Comfortable walking shoes for city sightseeing; hiking boots if planning Huangshan or Yangshuo countryside walks.
