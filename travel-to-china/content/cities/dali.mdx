 <div class="quick-summary">
 <p>Dali is the kind of place that makes you want to cancel your onward ticket. Set between the towering Cangshan Mountains and the vast expanse of Erhai Lake, this ancient Bai settlement has been enchanting travellers for decades — a magnet for artists, writers, and anyone seeking an escape from the pace of modern China.</p>
 <p>The <strong>Three Pagodas of Chongsheng Temple</strong> have stood guard over Dali for over 1,000 years. The old town's cobblestone lanes run with crystal-clear water diverted from Cangshan's mountain streams. And Erhai Lake — the "Ear-shaped Sea" — offers one of the most scenic cycling routes in China, with 120km of shoreline, fishing villages, and pagodas reflected in turquoise water.</p>
 </div>

 ## 📍 Where is Dali?
 
 Dali is located in <strong>western Yunnan Province</strong>, about 2 hours by high-speed rail from Kunming and 2 hours from Lijiang. It's the perfect midpoint on the classic Yunnan route: Kunming → Dali → Lijiang → Shangri-La.

 ## 🗺️ Attractions — Ranked
 
 ### 🔴 Must-See
 
 **1. Erhai Lake Cycling (洱海骑行)** — A 120km lakeside cycling route. The most popular 30km section runs from Dali Ancient Town to Xizhou, passing fishing villages, pagodas, and unbroken lake views. Bike rental ¥30-50/day. E-bike ¥60-80.
 
 **2. Three Pagodas of Chongsheng Temple (崇圣寺三塔)** — Three Tang Dynasty pagodas built between 824 and 840 AD. The main pagoda stands 69m tall. They have survived over 30 earthquakes. ¥75. 2h.
 
 **3. Dali Ancient Town (大理古城)** — A pedestrianised old town with Bai architecture, flowing streams, and a grid of streets filled with cafes, galleries, and craft shops. Free. 2-4h.
 
 ### 🟢 Worth a Visit
 
 **4. Cangshan Mountain Cable Car (苍山)** — Take the cable car from Dali to the top of Cangshan (3,800m). The view overlooking Erhai Lake from above the clouds is spectacular. The Gantong Cable Car (感通索道) is the most scenic. ¥120-200 round trip.
 
 **5. Xizhou Ancient Town (喜洲古镇)** — A Bai town 30 min north of Dali with some of the best-preserved Ming and Qing courtyard houses in Yunnan. Free. 2h.
 
 **6. Shuanglang Ancient Town (双廊古镇)** — A fishing village on Erhai's eastern shore. The view back toward Cangshan across the water is the most photographed in the Dali area. Free. 2h.

 ## 🍜 Food Guide
 
 Dali's cuisine blends Yunnan's fresh ingredients with Bai minority cooking traditions.
 
 **Erkuai (饵块)** — A Dali signature. Pounded rice cakes, sliced and stir-fried with vegetables and ham, or grilled with sweet bean paste. ¥10-20.
 
 **Crossing-the-Bridge Noodles (过桥米线)** — Yunnan's ceremonial dish (see Lijiang or Kunming guides for full description). Available throughout Dali. ¥28-58.
 
 **Rose Cake (鲜花饼)** — Flaky pastries filled with sweetened Yunnan roses. Dali has the best in the province. ¥5-8.
 
 **Yunnan Ham (诺邓火腿)** — Cured mountain ham from the nearby Nuodeng village. Thinly sliced and served cold. ¥30-60.
