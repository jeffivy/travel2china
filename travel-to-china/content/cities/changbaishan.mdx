 <div class="quick-summary">
 <p>Changbaishan (Ever-White Mountain) is the most revered peak in Northeast China — a dormant volcano whose summit crater holds Tianchi (Heaven Lake), one of the deepest alpine lakes in Asia. The lake straddles the border with North Korea, and on the rare days when the clouds clear, the water is a shade of turquoise that seems to belong to another planet.</p>
 <p>The mountain is sacred in both Korean and Manchu shamanic traditions — it is said that the first king of the Korean nation was born here. The Chinese name means "Ever-White Mountain," a reference to the long winter snow that blankets the peak for eight months of the year. The volcanically heated hot springs at the base offer a surreal soak surrounded by snow and steam in the winter months.</p>
 </div>

 ## 📍 Where is Changbaishan?
 
 Changbaishan is located in <strong>southeastern Jilin Province</strong>, on the border with North Korea. The nearest city is Yanji (1.5h by car) or Changbaishan Airport (NBS, 1.5h drive to the west entrance). Direct flights arrive from Beijing, Shanghai, and Changchun.

 ## 🗺️ Attractions — Ranked
 
 ### 🔴 Must-See
 
 **1. Tianchi (Heaven Lake / 天池)** — The crater lake at 2,189m. 13m deep, 4.7km circumference. Turquoise water surrounded by a ring of volcanic peaks. On average it's visible only 30-40 days per year — the rest is shrouded in mist. Visit in September for the best chance of clear skies. Accessible via the West Slope boardwalk (1,400 steps) or the North Slope jeep road.
 
 | Detail | Info |
 |--------|------|
 | Admission | ¥125 (park) + ¥85 (shuttle) + ¥80 (jeep to summit) |
 | Best approach | West Slope (fewer crowds, better views) |
 
 **2. Changbai Waterfall (长白瀑布)** — A 68m waterfall cascading from Tianchi through a volcanic notch. The water is ice-cold and milky white from rock flour. Accessible via a 30-min walk from the North Slope shuttle stop. Free (included in park admission).
 
 **3. Julong Hot Springs (聚龙温泉)** — Volcanic hot springs at the base of the waterfall. Water temperature reaches 82°C — hot enough to boil eggs. Steam rising through the forest on a winter morning is magical. ¥10 for egg dipping.
 
 ### 🟢 Worth a Visit
 
 **4. Heaven's Path (天阶)** — The West Slope's 1,400-step boardwalk to the Tianchi summit rim. Takes about 45 min to climb. The view from the top of the crater rim is the best in the park.
 
 **5. Green Deep Pool (绿渊潭)** — A small, impossibly green pool at the base of a cliff. Quick 20-min walk from the North Slope shuttle stop. Free.

 ## 🍜 Food Guide
 
 Changbaishan's food is heavily influenced by the Korean-Chinese culture of the Yanbian region.
 
 **Korean Cold Noodles (冷面)** — Buckwheat noodles in a cold beef broth with ice, sliced pear, cucumber, beef, and a boiled egg. Refreshing and addictive. ¥15-25.
 
 **Mountain Wild Vegetables (山野菜)** — Local foraged greens, stir-fried with garlic. Changes with the season. ¥15-30.
 
 **Hot Spring Boiled Eggs (温泉鸡蛋)** — Eggs boiled at 82°C in the volcanic hot springs. The yolk cooks solid while the white remains jelly-like. A unique texture. ¥10 for 3.

 ## 📱 Before You Go — Checklist
 
 **Tianchi luck:** Check the webcam feed before buying a ticket — if it's clouded over, wait for a clearer day
 **Warm clothing:** Summit is 5-10°C even in summer. Windproof jacket essential
 **Sunglasses:** UV at 2,189m + reflection from lake is intense
 **September:** Book accommodation 2+ weeks ahead — best weather month
