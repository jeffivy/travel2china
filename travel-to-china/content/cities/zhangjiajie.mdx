 <div class="quick-summary">
 <p>Zhangjiajie is home to one of the most otherworldly landscapes on Earth. Over 3,000 quartz-sandstone pillars rise from the forest floor like the spires of a submerged cathedral — some over 200 metres tall, with trees clinging to their summits as if planted by a god with a sense of theatre. When James Cameron's team designed the floating mountains of Pandora for <em>Avatar</em>, they came here for inspiration. The resemblance is uncanny.</p>
 <p>Beyond the national park, Zhangjiajie offers <strong>Tianmen Mountain</strong> — a sheer cliff with a natural arch called Heaven's Gate — the world's longest and highest glass-bottomed bridge, and the famous <strong>Bailong Elevator</strong> that rises 326 metres up the face of a sandstone cliff.</p>
 </div>

 ## 📍 Where is Zhangjiajie?
 
 Zhangjiajie is located in <strong>Northwest Hunan Province</strong>. It is served by a small airport with direct flights from Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and Xi'an. The high-speed train from Changsha takes about 3 hours. The city is often paired with Fenghuang Ancient Town (2 hours away) for a combined nature-culture itinerary.

 ## 🗺️ Attractions — Ranked
 
 ### 🔴 Must-See (Don't Miss These)
 
 **1. Zhangjiajie National Forest Park** — China's first UNESCO World Heritage national park (1982). The quartz-sandstone pillars that inspired Avatar's floating mountains. The park is divided into several zones:
 
 | Zone | Highlight | Cable Car / Elevator | Time Needed |
 |------|-----------|---------------------|-------------|
 | Yuanjiajie | "Avatar Hallelujah Mountain" — Southern Sky Column | Yes | 2-3h |
 | Tianzi Mountain | Panoramic views of the pillar forest | Cable car | 2-3h |
 | Yangjiajie | Quieter, wilder hiking trails | No (hike) | 3-4h |
 | Golden Whip Stream | 7.5 km valley trail along a crystal-clear stream | No (walk) | 2-3h |
 
 | Detail | Information |
 |--------|-------------|
 | **Open** | 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM (winter to 5 PM) |
 | **Admission** | ¥225 (4-day pass, includes park buses) |
 | **Getting there** | 30 min bus from city |
 | **Best time** | 7:00-9:00 AM before tour groups arrive |
 
 <div class="tip-box">
 🛎️ <strong>Park Strategy:</strong> Enter through the <strong>Forest Park Gate</strong> (south entrance), take the <strong>Bailong Elevator</strong> (¥65) up to Yuanjiajie first (before 8 AM to avoid 2-hour queues), then walk to Tianzi Mountain, take the cable car down, and end the day walking the Golden Whip Stream. This covers the three best zones in one day with minimum backtracking.
 </div>
 
 **2. Tianmen Mountain (Heaven's Gate Mountain)** — A 1,518-metre peak with a natural arch hole called Tianmen Cave (Heaven's Gate). Getting there is the experience: the world's longest cable car ride (7.5 km, 28 minutes) ascends from the city centre to the mountain top, climbing straight up the cliff face. At the top, the <strong>Glass Skywalk</strong> clings to a sheer vertical cliff — 60 metres of transparent floor 1,430 metres above the valley floor.
 
 | Detail | Information |
 |--------|-------------|
 | **Open** | 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM |
 | **Admission** | ¥258 (including cable car) |
 | **Getting there** | Cable car station is in the city centre |
 | **Best time** | Early morning for clear mountain views |
 | **Time needed** | Half day |
 
 <div class="warning-box">
 ⚠️ <strong>Glass Skywalk Tips:</strong> Required shoe covers (¥5) provided at entrance. No bags allowed on the skywalk — use the free lockers. If you are afraid of heights, try the middle section first (less exposed). The mist can obscure the view entirely — check the weather forecast and go on a clear day.
 </div>
 
 **3. Bailong Elevator (Hundred Dragons Elevator)** — The world's tallest outdoor elevator. 326 metres of glass-walled ascent straight up the face of a quartz-sandstone cliff. Built into the rock itself, the elevator rises from a valley floor to the top of Yuanjiajie in 1 minute 32 seconds. The view as you emerge above the pillar tops is one of the most dramatic arrivals in any national park on Earth.
 
 | Detail | Information |
 |--------|-------------|
 | **Open** | 7:30 AM - 5:30 PM |
 | **Admission** | ¥65 one way, ¥130 round trip |
 | **Getting there** | Inside the national park — bus from park gate |
 | **Best time** | Before 9:00 AM (queue can reach 2+ hours by noon) |
 
 ### 🟢 Worth a Visit (If You Have Time)
 
 **4. Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon Glass Bridge** — The world's longest and highest glass-bottomed bridge (430 metres long, 300 metres high). Transparent panels let you see the canyon floor between the quartz-sandstone walls. A bungee jump platform at the centre adds a heart-stopping option.
 
 | Detail | Information |
 |--------|-------------|
 | **Open** | 7:30 AM - 5:00 PM |
 | **Admission** | ¥299 (bridge + canyon) |
 | **Getting there** | 30 min bus from city |
 | **Time needed** | 2-3 hours |
 
 **5. Yellow Dragon Cave (Huanglong Dong)** — One of China's largest karst caves. A multi-level labyrinth of caverns, stalactites, and underground rivers. A boat ride through the cave lake is the highlight. ¥100. 2h.
 
 **6. Baofeng Lake** — A turquoise reservoir surrounded by sheer green cliffs. Pleasant boat ride and waterfall hike. ¥96. 2h.

 ## 🍜 Food Guide
 
 Zhangjiajie's cuisine is <strong>Xiangxi (West Hunan) cuisine</strong> — fiercer and smokier than the Hunan food you find in Changsha. Miao and Tujia minority influences dominate.
 
 **Sanxiaguo (Three-Bowl Pot / 三下锅)** — The signature Zhangjiajie dish. Three meats (pork, chicken, duck) and vegetables are stewed together in a clay pot over charcoal. The broth is dark, smoky, and layered. Every restaurant has its own version. ¥38-68.
 
 **Tuofeng Fish (Traffic Fish / 拖峰鱼)** — Local river fish steamed with pickled chilli peppers, ginger, and garlic. The fish is tender, the sauce is sour-savoury-spicy, and the whole thing is served in a wooden trough. ¥48-78.
 
 **Stinky Tofu (Choudoufu)** — Hunan's famous fermented tofu. Deep-fried until black and crispy on the outside, soft on the inside. Served with chilli sauce and pickled cabbage. ¥10-15.
 
 **Tujia Smoked Pork (土家腊肉)** — Pork belly cured over pine wood smoke for weeks. Sliced thin and stir-fried with garlic shoots or green peppers. Deep, primal flavour. ¥35-55.
 
 **Ge Fen (葛粉)** — A jelly made from kudzu root starch, served cold with honey, vinegar, and nuts. A refreshing dessert after all that spice. ¥8-15.
