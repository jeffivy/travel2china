 <div class="quick-summary">
 <p>Luoyang is one of China's great ancient capitals — a city that served as the imperial seat for 13 dynasties over 1,500 years. It is best known today for the <strong>Longmen Grottoes</strong>, a UNESCO World Heritage site where over 100,000 Buddhist statues were carved into the limestone cliffs along the Yi River between 493 and 1127 AD. The scale is staggering: the largest statue, Vairocana Buddha, stands 17 metres tall and was completed in 676 AD under the reign of Empress Wu Zetian.</p>
 <p>Beyond the grottoes, Luoyang is the home of the <strong>Shaolin Temple</strong> — the birthplace of Chan (Zen) Buddhism and kung fu — and the <strong>White Horse Temple</strong>, the first Buddhist temple established in China (68 AD). In spring, the entire city erupts in peony flowers, earning Luoyang its title as the "Peony Capital of China."</p>
 </div>

 ## 📍 Where is Luoyang?
 
 Luoyang is located in <strong>Henan Province</strong>, in the central plain that has been the cradle of Chinese civilisation for over 4,000 years. It is 1 hour by high-speed rail from Zhengzhou (the provincial capital) and 2.5 hours from Xi'an — making it an ideal stop on a historical route between Xi'an (Terracotta Warriors) and the ancient capitals of the Yellow River valley.

 ## 🗺️ Attractions — Ranked
 
 ### 🔴 Must-See (Don't Miss These)
 
 **1. Longmen Grottoes (龙门石窟)**
 
 The crown jewel of Luoyang. Over 2,300 caves and niches carved into a kilometre-long limestone cliff face along the Yi River. The sheer density of statuary — over 100,000 individual figures — makes it one of the most concentrated Buddhist art sites in the world. The centrepiece is the <strong>Fengxian Temple</strong> cave, featuring the 17-metre Vairocana Buddha, whose serene face is said to have been modelled on Empress Wu Zetian herself.
 
 | Detail | Information |
 |--------|-------------|
 | **Open** | 8:00 AM - 6:30 PM (Mar-Oct); 8:00 AM - 5:30 PM (Nov-Feb) |
 | **Admission** | ¥90 (Mar-Oct), ¥60 (Nov-Feb) |
 | **Getting there** | Bus 53, 60, or 81 from city centre (30 min) |
 | **Best time** | 8:00 AM (open) — before the tour groups flood in |
 | **Time needed** | 3-4 hours |
 
 <div class="tip-box">
 🛎️ <strong>Strategy:</strong> Enter from the west gate, walk along the west cliff (the main concentration of large caves), cross the bridge to the east side for the panoramic view back at the west cliff, then visit the smaller east-side caves and the Bai Yuan (poet's tomb) on the way out. The night illumination (¥40 extra, selected dates) transforms the grottoes into a magical scene.
 </div>
 
 **2. Shaolin Temple (少林寺)**
 
 The most famous Buddhist temple in China — the birthplace of Chan (Zen) Buddhism and the cradle of Chinese martial arts. Founded in 495 AD, the temple complex includes the original meditation hall, the Pagoda Forest (over 200 brick pagodas housing the ashes of past abbots), and the Kung Fu Training Centre where young monks practise from age 6. Daily kung fu performances are held at the training hall.
 
 | Detail | Information |
 |--------|-------------|
 | **Open** | 8:00 AM - 5:30 PM |
 | **Admission** | ¥100 (including kung fu performance) |
 | **Getting there** | Bus from Luoyang Train Station (1.5h) or taxi (1h, ¥100-150) |
 | **Best time** | 8:00 AM — the kung fu performance is at 10:30 AM |
 | **Time needed** | Full day (including travel) |
 
 <div class="tip-box">
 🛎️ <strong>Shaolin Strategy:</strong> The temple itself can be seen in 1.5 hours. The main reasons to spend the full day are: (1) the 10:30 AM kung fu performance (included, 40 min), (2) hiking up to the <strong>Damo Cave</strong> where Bodhidharma (the founder of Chan Buddhism) meditated for nine years, and (3) the <strong>Pagoda Forest</strong> — the most atmospheric part of the complex. Eat lunch at one of the vegetarian restaurants outside the temple gate (¥20-40).
 </div>
 
 **3. White Horse Temple (白马寺)**
 
 The first Buddhist temple in China, founded in 68 AD during the Eastern Han Dynasty. Named after the white horse that carried the first Buddhist sutras from India to China on its back. The temple still stands on its original site — over 1,950 years of continuous religious use. The international pagoda area includes Indian, Thai, and Burmese-style temples built by those countries as cultural gifts.
 
 | Detail | Information |
 |--------|-------------|
 | **Open** | 7:30 AM - 6:30 PM |
 | **Admission** | ¥35 |
 | **Getting there** | Bus 56 or 58 from Luoyang city centre (45 min) |
 | **Time needed** | 2-3 hours |
 
 ### 🟢 Worth a Visit (If You Have Time)
 
 **4. Luoyang Museum (洛阳博物馆)** — One of China's best prefectural museums. The permanent collection covers 5,000 years of Luoyang's history as a capital. The Bronze Age Hall with Shang and Zhou ritual vessels is exceptional. Free (reserve online). 2-3 hours.
 
 **5. Luoyang Old Town (洛阳老街)** — The historic quarter around the Drum Tower (Liyuan Gate). Narrow streets lined with traditional shops selling peony-related products, local snacks, and calligraphy supplies. Free. 2 hours. Best in the evening when lanterns are lit.
 
 **6. Luoyang National Peony Garden (国家牡丹园)** — The largest peony garden in China's peony capital. Over 400,000 peony plants representing 1,200 varieties. April is the peak bloom season. The city transforms into a sea of pink, red, white, and yellow flowers. ¥60. 2-3 hours.
 
 ### ⚪ Off the Beaten Path
 
 **7. Guanlin Temple (关林庙)** — The tomb of Guan Yu, the deified general of the Three Kingdoms period whose cult spread across East Asia. A serene temple complex with ancient cypress trees. ¥40. 1 hour.

 ## 🍜 Food Guide
 
 Luoyang's cuisine is Henan cuisine — hearty, wheat-based, and deeply connected to the agricultural heartland of China. The city's unique geography produces abundant freshwater fish from the Yellow River.
 
 **Luoyang Water Banquet (洛阳水席)** — The city's signature dining experience. A full-course banquet of 24 dishes served in sequence, mostly soup-based — hence "water banquet." The dishes alternate between cold appetisers and hot soups, building from mild to intensely flavoured. The most famous dish is <strong>Peony Swallow Dish (牡丹燕菜)</strong> — a delicate soup with shredded white radish that has been transformed to resemble swallow's nest. The water banquet is designed to be shared by 4-8 people. Full banquet ¥100-200 per person. Book at: <strong>Zhen Butong (真不同)</strong> restaurant on Zhongzhou Road — the most famous water banquet restaurant since 1895.
 
 **Luoyang Beef Soup (洛阳牛肉汤)** — The city's breakfast of champions. Beef bones are simmered overnight with star anise, cinnamon, and Sichuan peppercorn. The resulting broth is poured over hand-pulled noodles or served with sesame flatbread (shaobing). ¥8-15. Eat at: <strong>Ma Jie Beef Soup (马杰山牛肉汤)</strong> near the old town.
 
 **Gluten Baozi (Gluten Bun / 面筋包子)** — Wheat gluten is braised in a savoury broth, then stuffed into steamed buns with chopped scallions and served with a dipping sauce of vinegar and chilli oil. Soft, springy, and intensely satisfying. ¥10-15.
 
 **Yellow River Carp (黄河鲤鱼)** — Sweetwater carp from the Yellow River, which Luoyang overlooks. Steamed or braised in a sweet-sour sauce with shredded ginger and spring onions. ¥48-88.
 
 **Peony Pastry (牡丹饼)** — A delicate pastry filled with sweetened peony petals. The flower flavour is floral, subtle, and surprisingly sophisticated. Available everywhere during the Peony Festival season (April). ¥2-5 per piece.

 ## 🚌 Getting Around
 
 | Method | Notes |
 |--------|-------|
 | Metro | Line 1 and 2 — connect Longmen Station, city centre, and main tourist areas |
 | Bus | ¥1-2. Routes 53/60/81 to Longmen Grottoes. Routes 56/58 to White Horse Temple |
 | DiDi / Taxi | ¥10-20 within city. ¥100-150 to Shaolin Temple (1h) |
 
 **From Luoyang Longmen HSR Station:** Metro Line 2 to city centre (20 min), or taxi ¥20 to Longmen Grottoes (10 min).
 
 **Note:** There is no airport in Luoyang. Most visitors arrive by high-speed rail from Zhengzhou (40 min), Xi'an (2.5h), or Beijing (3.5h). The nearest major airport is Zhengzhou (CGO).

 <div class="tip-box">
 🛎️ <strong>Tang Sancai (唐三彩):</strong> Luoyang is the birthplace of Tang Sancai — the three-colour glazed pottery of the Tang Dynasty. The authentic pieces from local artisan workshops are a world apart from the factory reproductions sold at tourist shops. Visit the Luoyang Sancai Art Museum or the artisan district near the old town for the real thing.
