 <div class="quick-summary">
 <p>Sanya is China's only tropical beach destination — the equivalent of Phuket or Bali but without leaving Chinese territory. Located at the southern tip of Hainan Island (at the same latitude as Hawaii), Sanya offers white sand beaches, coral reefs, rainforest hiking, and luxury resorts at prices well below comparable destinations in Southeast Asia.</p>
 <p>Unlike most Chinese cities that are about history and culture, Sanya is about one thing: relaxation. Yalong Bay (亚龙湾) is the most beautiful beach — a 7km crescent of fine white sand and turquoise water. The rest of the area offers snorkelling at Wuzhizhou Island, Buddhist culture at Nanshan Temple, and jungle zip-lining at Yanoda Rainforest.</p>
 </div>

 ## 📍 Where is Sanya?
 
 Sanya sits at the southern tip of <strong>Hainan Island</strong>, China's southernmost province. It's a 2-hour flight from Beijing, 2.5 hours from Shanghai, and 1 hour from Guangzhou and Hong Kong. Hainan has a visa-free policy for citizens of 59 countries for stays up to 30 days — making it one of the most accessible destinations in China.

 ## 🗺️ Attractions — Ranked
 
 ### 🔴 Must-See (Don't Miss These)
 
 **1. Yalong Bay (亚龙湾)** — The best beach in Sanya (and arguably all of China). 7km of powdery white sand, turquoise water, and a mountain backdrop. Public beach access at Yalong Bay Central Square (free). Resort day passes available (¥100-300 for pool + beach access).
 
 **2. Wuzhizhou Island (蜈支洲岛)** — A tropical island 25 min by ferry from Sanya. Crystal-clear water, coral reefs, and world-class snorkelling. The island has a 3.5km boardwalk around the coastline. ¥140 (ferry + admission). Snorkelling gear rental ¥50-100. 4-5h.
 
 **3. Nanshan Temple & Guan Yin Statue (南山寺)** — A Buddhist cultural park with a 108-metre-tall Guan Yin (Avalokitesvara) statue standing in the sea — one of the tallest statues in the world. The temple complex is beautiful. ¥129. 3-4h.
 
 ### 🟢 Worth a Visit (If You Have Time)
 
 **4. Yanoda Rainforest Cultural Tourism Zone (呀诺达雨林)** — A tropical rainforest with zip-lining, waterfall hiking, and a glass-bottomed suspension bridge. ¥158. 3-4h.
 
 **5. Tianya Haijiao (天涯海角)** — "The Edge of the Sky and the Corner of the Sea." A scenic beach park with famous rocks and romantic significance in Chinese culture. ¥81. 2h. Tourist-heavy but has genuine coastal beauty.
 
 **6. Luhuitou Park (鹿回头)** — A hilltop park overlooking Sanya Bay. The best sunset viewpoint in the city. The deer statue is the symbol of Sanya. ¥45. 1.5-2h.

 ## 🍜 Food Guide
 
 Hainan cuisine is distinct from mainland Chinese food — lighter, more tropical, heavy on seafood and coconut.
 
 **Wenchang Chicken (文昌鸡)** — The most famous Hainan dish. Free-range chicken is poached in a light broth, served cold with ginger-garlic dipping sauce and rice cooked in chicken fat (like Hainanese chicken rice). ¥30-60.
 
 **Hainan Seafood Platter** — The Sanya seafood market (第一市场) lets you choose live seafood, then take it to a restaurant to be cooked. Pick your own: lobsters, crabs, prawns, clams, and local fish. ¥50-100 per person for the full experience.
 
 **Coconut Rice (椰子饭)** — Glutinous rice cooked inside a young coconut with coconut milk, sugar, and dried fruits. Sweet, creamy, and tropical. ¥15-25.
 
 **Qingbu Liang (清补凉)** — Sanya's definitive hot-weather dessert. Shaved ice with coconut milk, red beans, taro, coconut jelly, and fresh fruit. ¥10-15.
