 <div class="quick-summary">
 <p>Lijiang is unlike any other city in China. The old town — a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997 — is a labyrinth of cobblestone lanes, tiled roofs, and crystal-clear streams fed by meltwater from the nearby mountains. The Naxi people, descendants of Tibetan-speaking tribes who once controlled the Tea Horse Road trade route, have preserved a culture that blends Han Chinese, Tibetan, and indigenous traditions into something wholly unique.</p>
 <p>Above the ancient rooftops towers <strong>Jade Dragon Snow Mountain (Yulong Xueshan)</strong>, a 5,596-metre peak that has never been fully climbed. To the Naxi, it is a sacred guardian. To travellers, it is the backdrop to one of the most beautiful settings in all of Asia.</p>
 </div>

 ## 📍 Where is Lijiang?
 
 Lijiang is located in <strong>Northwest Yunnan Province</strong>, at an elevation of 2,400 metres. It is a 3-hour high-speed rail ride from Kunming, 2 hours from Dali, and serves as the gateway to Yunnan's legendary "Shangri-La" loop.

 ## 🗺️ Attractions — Ranked
 
 ### 🔴 Must-See (Don't Miss These)
 
 **1. Lijiang Old Town (Dayan)** — UNESCO World Heritage. An 800-year-old maze of cobblestone streets, flowing canals, and Naxi courtyard houses. Free entry. 24/7 access.
 
 **2. Black Dragon Pool (Heilong Tan)** — The iconic reflection of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain in a serene pond with the Moon-Embracing Pavilion. Best at sunrise. Free.
 
 **3. Jade Dragon Snow Mountain** — A sacred 5,596-metre massif. Take the Spruce Meadow cable car (4,506m) or the glacier cable car (4,680m, highest in Asia). ¥100 + cable car ¥120-180.
 
 <div class="warning-box">
 ⚠️ <strong>Altitude Caution:</strong> The glacier cable car reaches 4,680m. Spend a night in Lijiang (2,400m) first to acclimatise. Portable oxygen cans (¥20-30) are sold at the base.
 </div>
 
 ### 🟢 Worth a Visit (If You Have Time)
 
 **4. Shuhe Ancient Town** — Quieter sibling of Dayan. A key stop on the Tea Horse Road. Less commercialised. 20 min north. Free.
 
 **5. Mu Palace (Mufu)** — The former mansion of the Naxi Mu chieftains who ruled for 470 years. Gardens and architecture blend Naxi and Ming styles. ¥60.
 
 **6. Naxi Ancient Music Concert** — Tang Dynasty court music preserved by the Naxi. 8 PM daily at Dongba Music Hall. ¥160-180. 1 hour.
 
 ### ⚪ Off the Beaten Path
 
 **7. Baisha Ancient Town** — The oldest of Lijiang's three old towns. Baisha Murals combine Tibetan Buddhist, Naxi, and Han painting. 20 min north.
 
 **8. Yuhu Village** — The former home of Joseph Rock, the explorer who introduced Yunnan to the West.

 ## 🍜 Food Guide
 
 Lijiang's food is Naxi-Yunnan cuisine — influenced by Tibetan highland ingredients and Tea Horse Road spices.
 
 **Crossing-the-Bridge Noodles (Guoqiao Mixian)** — Yunnan's ceremonial dish. A bowl of boiling chicken broth arrives with a tray of raw ingredients (meat, vegetables, noodles) that you cook yourself at the table. ¥38-68.
 
 **Naxi Baba (粑粑)** — Signature flatbread. Thick, pan-fried wheat cake, sweet or savoury. ¥8-15.
 
 **Yunnan Cured Ham (Nuodeng Ham)** — Cured 3-5 years at altitude. Translucent, ruby-red. China's best prosciutto. ¥40-80.
 
 **Grilled Tilapia in Chilli Sauce** — Fresh fish grilled over charcoal, served sizzling with cumin and chilli. ¥38-58.
 
 **Yak Yogurt** — From nearby Tibetan plateau settlements. Thick, tangy, rich. ¥10-15.
