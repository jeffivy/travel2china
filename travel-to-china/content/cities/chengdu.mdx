## 📍 Where is Chengdu?

Chengdu is the hub of **Southwest China**. A region unto itself - it is a 3.5-hour high-speed rail ride from Xi'an, 1.5 hours from Chongqing, and a short flight from Yunnan's natural wonders. It pairs naturally with Chongqing (food route), Xi'an (history + food route), and Guilin (nature extension).

| to City | Transport | Time |
|---------|-----------|------|
| Chongqing | High-speed rail | 1.5h |

## 🗺️ Attractions - Ranked

### 🔴 Must-See (Don't Miss These)

**1. Giant Panda Breeding Research Base**
The reason most travelers fly to Chengdu. Home to over **200 giant pandas** in spacious, forested enclosures. This is not a zoo - it is a conservation center. The newborn cubs in the nursery are a highlight.

![Baby giant panda cub clinging to a wooden platform at morning feeding time](/images/chengdu-panda-cubs.jpg)

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Open** | 7:30 AM - 6:00 PM (last tickets 5:00 PM) |
| **Admission** | ¥55 (Mar-Nov), ¥53 (Dec-Feb) |
| **Getting there** | Metro Line 3 to Panda Avenue &rarr; shuttle bus or taxi (10 min) |
| **Best time** | **Before 8:00 AM** - feeding 8:00-9:30 AM, only active window |
| **Time needed** | 3-4 hours |

<div class="tip-box">
🛎️ <strong>Panda Pro Strategy:</strong> Head to the <strong>Moonlight Nursery</strong> first (newborn cubs in incubators), then <strong>Sunshine Nursery</strong> (6-12 month cubs wrestling - the highlight), then the adult enclosures. By 10:30 AM they are all asleep. <strong>Avoid weekends and Chinese holidays.</strong> Buy tickets from the official booth or WeChat mini-program - do not buy from touts at the gate.
</div>

**2. Kuanzhai Alley (宽窄巷子)**
Three Qing Dynasty alleyways transformed into a pedestrian quarter. Kuan Alley for teahouses; Zhai Alley for shopping and street food; Jing Alley with historical brickwork. Free entry. Metro Line 4. 2-3 hours.

**3. Sichuan Hot Pot Dinner**
Not a tourist activity - a rite of passage. See [Food Guide &darr;](#food-guide).

### 🟢 Worth a Visit (If You Have Time)

**4. Wuhou Shrine (武侯祠)**
Dedicated to Zhuge Liang. The red-walled bamboo corridor is extremely photogenic - arrive before 10 AM to avoid the queue. ¥60. Metro Line 3 to Gaoshengqiao. Adjacent to Jinli Street. 1.5-2 hours.

**5. Jinli Ancient Street (锦里古街)**
Lantern-strung pedestrian lane. Visit at **night** when the red lanterns transform it into the most atmospheric evening walk in the city. Free. 1-2 hours.

**6. People's Park (人民公园) & Teahouse**
The heart of Chengdu's slow-living culture. Sit in a bamboo chair with jasmine tea (¥20-30), watch locals play mahjong. Free entry. Metro Line 2. 1-3 hours.

### ⚪ Off the Beaten Path

**7. Shu Embroidery Museum (蜀绣博物馆)** - Free. Near Qingyang Palace.
**8. Qingyang Palace (青羊宫)** - Tang dynasty Taoist temple. ¥10. Metro Line 5.

### ❌ Skip These (Tourist Traps)

| Trap | Why to Skip |
|------|-------------|
| Jinli Street restaurants | Overpriced mediocre food. Visit for atmosphere, eat elsewhere |
| Rickshaw rides around Kuanzhai | ¥100 for a 15-min loop |
| Panda Base VIP touts | Fake. Buy from official booth only |
| Wild mountain fungus shops | ¥1,000+/jin targeting tourists |

## 🍜 Food Guide

Chengdu is a **UNESCO City of Gastronomy**. The defining flavor is **mala** (麻辣): Sichuan peppercorn numbing + chili heat.

### Spice Survival Guide
Order **weila** (微辣 / mild spicy). In Chengdu, "mild" is hotter than most cuisines' "extra hot." Ask for a half-and-half pot (鸳鸯锅) with clear broth on one side. Never drink the hot pot broth - it is a cooking medium. Your coolant: sesame oil + garlic + cilantro.

| Dish | What It Is | Where to Eat | Price |
|------|------------|-------------|-------|
| Sichuan Hot Pot | Bubbling chili broth, dip ingredients to cook | Shu Daxia, Xiaolongkan, Huangcheng Laoma | ¥80-150 |
| Chuan Chuan Xiang | Hot pot on a stick, pay by skewer | Ma Lu Bian Bian | ¥30-60 |
| Dan Dan Noodles | Wheat noodles + minced pork + chili oil | Xiao Tan Douhua | ¥12-18 |
| Mapo Tofu | Silken tofu in chili bean sauce | Chen Mapo Tofu (since 1862) | ¥28-38 |
| Long Chaoshou | Dragon wontons in savory broth | Long Chaoshou on Chunxi Road | ¥15-25 |
| Rabbit Head (兔头) | Braised rabbit head - midnight snack | Shuangliu Laoma Tutou | ¥12-18 |
